The mobile app for planning and coordinating your group trips together.

Alela brings together, in a single app, everything a group needs to prepare, decide and share — to make organizing a trip with others simple.

Free to download, no subscription, on iOS and Android.

01Our vision

Less time organizing, more time enjoying it.

A trip with friends, the yearly family reunion, Margaux's 30th birthday, Antoine's bachelor party… Getting together as a group sounds pretty great on paper.

And then comes the organizing phase. In every group, there are usually the ones who plan everything (finding the lodging, chasing everyone up, thinking about travel, doing the maths…) and the ones who, on departure day, find 100 unread messages and feel guilty for letting Marie handle it all again.

Until now, we always managed without a dedicated app. But we figured we could make it simpler, clearer and more fun for everyone. With Alela, you can plan, decide, find, share and follow up; and then, ideally, close the app to spend less time organizing and more time enjoying your trip!

03Features

For every trip, one dedicated space.

Every trip has its own space, shared by the whole group and synced in real time.

You'll find every feature there: from dates to budget, through meals, luggage and memories, plus decisions, practical info, the group conversation and much more!

Of course, for each trip you only use what's actually useful to you, and you can create as many trips as you like!

+

Click or hover over the icons to discover each feature in detail.Tap an icon to discover each feature in detail.

Dates

Find when to leave, without spending three weeks on it.

Two ways to do it: set the dates directly to let participants know, or decide together with a poll to factor in everyone's availability and preferences, without endless back-and-forth.

Once the dates are set, the info is right there at a glance, and everyone can add the trip to their own calendar.

Destinations

Choose the destination everyone can agree on.

Here too, it's your call: the destination can be set directly to let participants know, or decided together with a poll: everyone suggests, the group votes.

Then everyone can easily find the info, along with the map and weather for the destination.

Going on a road trip? No problem: enter several destinations, each with its own dates.

Lodging

Find everyone a place to stay, no headaches.

Already got your lodging? Share the useful info (booking link, address, arrival, parking…): the group finds it in a heartbeat, and anything to pack goes straight into the luggage checklist. If not everyone's staying in the same place, just add several lodgings.

Haven't found one yet? Alela makes your search easier across multiple platforms (Airbnb, Booking, Agoda…) by pre-filling your criteria: dates, location, number of participants. Save your favorites, then launch a poll to help the group decide: price, location, participant reviews…

Travel

Coordinate your travel and get there together.

The idea: no one's left without a way to get to the trip, and cars don't leave half-empty…

To make that happen, everyone enters their itinerary and the details of their outbound/return trip, even if it's not fully set yet.

From there, Alela spots possible coordination and suggests shared trips between participants when it makes sense: carpooling, a detour to pick someone up, or simply taking the same train to enjoy the journey together.

Suggestions take everyone's real situation into account: mode of transport, available seats, starting and end points, possible detours, flexibility on dates and times…

Meals

Feed a group, without the headache.

Get a meal planner for the trip: everyone sees the planned meal day by day, with quantities adjusted to who's actually there.

Short on menu ideas? Alela suggests meal ideas and full recipes, scaled to the number of guests. And for suggestions that really fit, everyone can fill in their food profile: allergies, diet, foods to avoid. Suggestions can then take that into account without you having to think about it.

Not sure about the menu? You can also launch polls to make sure the meals really work for everyone, and save your favorite recipes for your next trips.

Shopping

A shopping list generated in a heartbeat.

A shared shopping list for the group, organized by aisle and by meal: anyone can add items, and at the store, everyone checks off from their own phone, with the list updating live for everyone.

And if you've filled in the meal planner, the shopping list builds itself. Every ingredient from the planned recipes gets added, in the right quantities for the right number of guests, without typing a thing.

Luggage

Suitcases ready, without even thinking about it.

Everyone gets their own checklist to pack with peace of mind!

Its biggest strength: it pre-configures itself and updates based on the trip's context (duration, weather, country and culture, lodging essentials, mode of transport and more), while adapting to your profile and choices: planned activities, luggage space available, personal essentials, pets…

Traveling with your kids? You'll also find their lists, tailored to their age and the trip's context. And if the other co-parent is part of the trip, the list is shared between you.

And for items useful to everyone (a speaker, games, the first-aid kit…), a shared group list brings it all together. Anyone can claim an item to bring: no duplicates… and no more forgetting things!

Budget

Zero end-of-trip math.

Paid for something for the group, or for some of the participants? Add it in Alela, and see in real time who owes what to whom! Alela suggests the simplest way to settle up between participants at the end of the trip, and each reimbursement gets marked off in one tap.

Paid in different currencies? No worries: Alela converts automatically to make things easier for you.

Bonus: the cost of the lodging entered in Alela, and the €4.99 to unlock Adventure Mode, can be imported into the budget automatically, to be split directly between all participants!

Memories

Everyone's photos, in one place.

A shared space for everyone's photos and videos, with reactions and comments to relive the moments together. Everything downloads in one tap, with no duplicates, and each trip keeps its own album, to reopen months later.

Chat

A conversation that brings the whole group together automatically!

As soon as the trip is created, the group conversation already exists, with every participant already in it. Nothing to set up on another app, no phone numbers or contacts to collect.

Need to chat one-on-one? Create conversations with just the participants you choose: a surprise gift to plan without the person concerned, a carpool to sort out between two people…

Polls

The group votes, the decision is made.

Dates, destinations, lodging, meal ideas… Whenever something needs deciding, launch a poll: everyone votes from their phone, the result is visible to all, and the decision is made, with no chasing or endless debate.

Converter

Prices on the spot, in your currency.

On a trip abroad? Convert a price into your usual currency in two seconds!

05Frequently asked questions

We've got answers.

Is this only for travel?
Not at all! Alela is designed for every occasion to get together as a group: weekends with friends, family vacations, bachelor/bachelorette parties, family reunions, festivals, corporate retreats… No need to go far, or for long: as soon as a group needs to plan and get organized, Alela is there to help you spend less time coordinating, and more time enjoying your trip!
When should you use Alela?
Throughout the whole trip!
  • Before, to decide and prepare: dates, lodging, travel, packing…
  • During, to live the trip: expenses, meals, shopping, chat…
  • After, to settle up and share your memories.
Can you organize several trips at the same time?
Yes. Every trip has its own space with all of Alela's features (participants, chat, budget, photos, etc.). You can switch between trips from the "My trips" screen. The number of trips is unlimited, even in the free mode.
How do participants join a trip?
Once a trip is created, an invite link and a QR code are generated. Just share them with participants however you like (WhatsApp, text, email…). Anyone who taps it joins the trip directly in the app.
How many participants can you invite?
As many as you need: a small weekend for 4 or a big family reunion for 20, the app doesn't cap the number of participants in a trip.
Do you need to create an account?
Yes, but it's quick: an email address is all it takes.
What if a participant hasn't downloaded Alela yet?
No problem: the invite link first redirects them to the App Store or Play Store to install Alela. Once the app is installed and their account created, they just reopen the invite link to join the trip.
Is the app available in my language?
Alela is available in 9 languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Norwegian, Swedish and Dutch. The app automatically displays in your phone's language, and everyone can choose their own: within the same trip, each participant can use Alela in their own language!
Is the app free?
Yes, the app is free to download. Then, for every new trip, you have two options:
  • Getaway mode, entirely free;
  • Adventure mode, which unlocks the advanced features, for €4.99 per trip, paid once.
Good news: during the launch period, Adventure Mode is free on all your new trips!
Is it a subscription?
No. Adventure Mode is a one-time purchase per trip: you pay €4.99 once, nothing renews, nothing to cancel.
Who pays to unlock Adventure Mode?
Adventure Mode can be unlocked:
  • either when the trip is created, by the person who creates it;
  • or later, at any time, by any participant who wants to switch from Getaway to Adventure to unlock the advanced features.
Either way, a single payment of €4.99 is enough, and every participant benefits right away. The person who paid can then add the amount to the trip budget to split it: for six people, that's less than a coffee each ☕
What's included in the free mode (Getaway)?
Everything you need to organize a trip with others, with no limit on duration or number of trips. To see exactly what each mode includes, compare the two modes feature by feature.
What happens to the photos/videos after the trip?
They stay stored in the trip, accessible to all its participants: handy for reliving your memories months later. You can also download everything to your phone in one tap, with no duplicates.
Is my data private?
Yes. A trip's content (photos, messages, expenses, etc.) is only visible to the trip's participants. Details in our privacy policy.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, at any time: you can permanently delete your account from the app, in Profile → Account & security.
